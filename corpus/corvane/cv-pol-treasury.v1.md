# Treasury Policy

This Treasury Policy sets the rules under which Corvane Freight Holdings ("Corvane") holds cash, places surplus funds and authorises payments. It applies to all Corvane group entities and is owned by the Treasury Lead.

## 1. Liquidity

1.1 Corvane shall hold unrestricted cash of at least USD 25,000,000 at all times, measured at the close of each business day.

1.2 If unrestricted cash falls below the minimum, the Treasury Lead shall notify the Chief Financial Officer within one (1) business day and present a restoration plan within five (5) business days.

## 2. Counterparty Limits

2.1 No more than twenty percent (20%) of unrestricted cash may be placed with any single bank or money-market counterparty.

2.2 Only counterparties rated at or above the internal investment-grade threshold may hold surplus funds. The approved counterparty list is reviewed every six (6) months.

## 3. Investment of Surplus Funds

3.1 Surplus funds may be placed only in bank deposits and money-market instruments with a maximum tenor of ninety (90) days.

3.2 No investment may be made in equities, commodities or instruments whose value can fall below the amount invested.

## 4. Payment Authorisation

4.1 Any single payment above USD 5,000,000 requires two authorised signatories, one of whom must be the Treasury Lead or the Chief Financial Officer.

4.2 Payment instructions to a new beneficiary must be verified by a call-back to a known contact before the first payment is released.

## 5. Reporting

5.1 The Treasury Lead issues a weekly cash position report to the Chief Financial Officer and a monthly liquidity report to the Board Finance Committee.

5.2 Exceptions to this Policy must be approved in writing by the Chief Financial Officer and recorded in the exceptions register, which is reviewed quarterly.

## 6. Review

6.1 This Policy is reviewed at least once every twenty-four (24) months or after any material change to Corvane's funding arrangements.

6.2 Changes to this Policy take effect only when approved by the Board Finance Committee and published as a new version.

## 7. Roles

7.1 The Chief Financial Officer is accountable for the Policy. The Treasury Lead operates it day to day and keeps the bank mandate register, the counterparty list and the exceptions register.

7.2 Internal Audit tests compliance with this Policy once a year and reports its findings to the Board Finance Committee.
