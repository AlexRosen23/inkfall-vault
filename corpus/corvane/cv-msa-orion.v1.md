# Master Services Agreement — Orion Telematics Ltd.

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Corvane Freight Holdings ("Corvane") and Orion Telematics Ltd. ("Orion") and takes effect on 1 March 2023 (the "Effective Date").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Platform" means the fleet telematics, route-tracking and geofencing service operated by Orion and made available to Corvane depots under this Agreement.

1.2 "Fees" means the monthly platform fee and per-vehicle fees set out in the Pricing Schedule, as updated from time to time by written schedule signed by both parties.

1.3 "Service Level Agreement" means the service level terms agreed between the parties for the Platform, including the uptime commitment and the service credit regime.

## 2. Term and Renewal

2.1 The initial term of this Agreement is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date.

2.2 This Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

2.3 A notice of non-renewal must be sent to the notice address stated in Section 9 and is effective on receipt by the other party.

## 3. Services and Service Levels

3.1 Orion shall provide the Platform in accordance with the Service Level Agreement and shall use reasonably skilled personnel and industry-standard security controls.

3.2 Orion shall give Corvane at least ten (10) business days' notice of any scheduled maintenance that falls outside the maintenance window stated in the Service Level Agreement.

## 4. Payment

4.1 Orion shall invoice the Fees monthly in arrears. Corvane shall pay each undisputed invoice within net thirty (30) days of receipt.

4.2 Disputed amounts must be notified within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve any dispute within thirty (30) days.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

5.1 Subject to clause 5.2, the total aggregate liability of each party arising under this Agreement in any contract year shall not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the Fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

5.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or breach of the confidentiality obligations in Section 7.

## 6. Termination

6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of receiving notice describing the breach.

6.2 On termination Orion shall return Corvane data in a standard export format within twenty (20) business days and then delete remaining copies.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret, use it only for this Agreement, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent duties.

## 8. Governing Law

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Westmarch, and the courts of Westmarch have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.

## 9. Notices

9.1 Notices must be in writing and delivered to the legal department of the receiving party at its registered office, with a copy by email to the contract owner named in the Pricing Schedule.
