# Amendment No. 1 to the Orion Telematics Master Services Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 (the "Amendment") is made between Corvane Freight Holdings ("Corvane") and Orion Telematics Ltd. ("Orion") and takes effect on 15 January 2026 (the "Amendment Date"). It amends the Restated Master Services Agreement that took effect on 1 March 2025 (the "Agreement").

## 1. Background

1.1 Corvane's finance team has moved to a consolidated payment run, and the parties agreed to lengthen the payment period for undisputed invoices in exchange for Corvane continuing to pay by electronic transfer.

## 2. Amendment to Clause 4.1

2.1 Clause 4.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following: "Orion shall invoice the Fees monthly in arrears. Corvane shall pay each undisputed invoice within net forty-five (45) days of receipt."

2.2 For the avoidance of doubt, invoices received before the Amendment Date remain payable within the period stated in the Agreement before this Amendment.

## 3. No Other Changes

3.1 Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, all terms of the Agreement, including the term, renewal, liability and service level provisions, continue in full force and effect.

3.2 In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment prevails as to the subject matter of clause 4.1 only.

## 4. General

4.1 This Amendment may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.

4.2 This Amendment is governed by the laws of the Province of Westmarch, consistent with Section 8 of the Agreement.

4.3 Signed for Corvane by its Group Procurement Director and for Orion by its Chief Commercial Officer on 12 January 2026.

## 5. Representations

5.1 Each party confirms that it has full authority to enter into this Amendment, that the individuals signing for it are duly authorised, and that no consent of any third party is needed.

5.2 Corvane confirms that its accounts payable team has been instructed to apply the revised payment period to all invoices dated on or after the Amendment Date, and Orion confirms that its billing desk has updated the payment terms printed on its invoices.

5.3 Neither party waives any accrued right or remedy under the Agreement by entering into this Amendment, and no course of dealing changes the meaning of any other clause.
