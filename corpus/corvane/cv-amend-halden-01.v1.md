# Amendment No. 1 to the Halden Route Analytics Master Services Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 (the "Amendment") is made between Corvane Freight Holdings ("Corvane") and Halden Route Analytics Ltd. ("Halden") and takes effect on 1 February 2026 (the "Amendment Date"). It amends the Master Services Agreement that took effect on 1 September 2024 (the "Agreement").

## 1. Background

1.1 Following two load-planning outages in the fourth quarter of 2025 that delayed morning dispatch at three Corvane depots, the parties agreed to raise the availability commitment for the Platform in return for a modest extension of the notice window for fee adjustments.

## 2. Amendment to Clause 4.1

2.1 Clause 4.1 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following: "Halden shall provide the Platform with a monthly availability of 99.8%, measured at the Halden application gateway and excluding scheduled maintenance."

2.2 The service credit regime in clause 4.2 of the Agreement continues to apply, measured against the availability level in clause 4.1 as amended.

## 3. Amendment to Clause 3.2

3.1 Halden shall give Corvane at least sixty (60) days' written notice of any adjustment to the monthly platform fee under clause 3.2 of the Agreement. The three percent (3%) annual ceiling is unchanged.

## 4. No Other Changes

4.1 Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, all terms of the Agreement continue in full force and effect.

4.2 In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment prevails as to clauses 3.2 and 4.1 only.

## 5. General

5.1 This Amendment may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.

5.2 This Amendment is governed by the laws of the Province of Westmarch, consistent with clause 7.2 of the Agreement.

## 6. Representations

6.1 Each party confirms that it has full authority to enter into this Amendment, that the individuals signing for it are duly authorised, and that no consent of any third party is needed.

6.2 Halden confirms that the revised availability commitment has been configured in its internal monitoring, and that measurement of the new commitment starts on the Amendment Date, not on the first day of the following month.

6.3 Neither party waives any accrued right or remedy under the Agreement by entering into this Amendment.
