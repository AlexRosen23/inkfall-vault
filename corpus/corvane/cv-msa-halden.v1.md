# Master Services Agreement — Halden Route Analytics Ltd.

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Corvane Freight Holdings ("Corvane") and Halden Route Analytics Ltd. ("Halden") and takes effect on 1 September 2024 (the "Effective Date").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Platform" means the route optimisation and load-planning analytics service operated by Halden and made available to Corvane depots under this Agreement.

1.2 "Fees" means the monthly platform fee of USD 27,800 and any usage fees set out in the Halden Order Form.

1.3 "Service Level Agreement" means the service level terms in Section 4 of this Agreement.

## 2. Term and Renewal

2.1 The initial term of this Agreement is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date.

2.2 This Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Payment

3.1 Halden shall invoice the Fees monthly in arrears. Corvane shall pay each undisputed invoice within net thirty (30) days of receipt.

3.2 Halden may adjust the monthly platform fee on each anniversary of the Effective Date by no more than three percent (3%).

## 4. Service Levels

4.1 Halden shall provide the Platform with a monthly availability of 99.5%, measured at the Halden application gateway and excluding scheduled maintenance.

4.2 If monthly availability falls below the level in clause 4.1, Halden shall credit three percent (3%) of the monthly platform fee for each full half-percentage point of shortfall, up to a maximum credit of fifteen percent (15%).

4.3 Halden shall respond to a critical incident within thirty (30) minutes and shall issue a written incident summary within five (5) business days.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

5.1 Subject to clause 5.2, the total aggregate liability of each party arising under this Agreement in any contract year shall not exceed two hundred percent (200%) of the Fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

5.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or breach of the confidentiality obligations in Section 6.

## 6. Confidentiality and Data

6.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret and use it only for this Agreement. Halden shall process Corvane route and load data only to provide the Platform and shall not use it to train models offered to other customers.

## 7. Termination and Governing Law

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of receiving notice describing the breach.

7.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Westmarch, and the courts of Westmarch have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
