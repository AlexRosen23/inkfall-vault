# Incident Note INC-2026-0914 — Orion Telematics Platform Outage

This note records Corvane's own view of the telematics outage of 14 September 2026, compiled by the operations desk from depot logs and the dispatch console. It is an internal record and is not a statement agreed with the vendor.

## 1. Summary

1.1 On 14 September 2026 the Orion Telematics Ltd. Platform became unavailable to all Corvane depots. Vehicle positions and geofence alerts stopped updating on the dispatch console.

1.2 The outage began at 02:12 UTC and ended at 02:59 UTC, a total duration of 47 minutes, based on the first and last failed heartbeat entries in the dispatch console log.

1.3 Corvane raised a Severity 1 incident with the Orion support desk at 02:16 UTC. Orion acknowledged at 02:27 UTC.

## 2. Impact

2.1 A total of 14 depots lost live tracking. Morning departures at the Northgate and Harbourside depots were held for up to twenty (20) minutes while drivers were manually checked out.

2.2 No cargo loss, customer penalty or safety event was recorded. Eleven (11) scheduled customer notifications were sent late.

## 3. Response

3.1 The operations desk switched affected depots to manual check-in at 02:20 UTC and returned to normal procedures at 03:10 UTC after confirming position updates.

3.2 The duty manager kept a call open with Orion's support lead until service was restored.

## 4. Service Credit

4.1 Corvane calculated September availability at 99.89% on the basis of the 47-minute outage, which falls into the first tier of the service credit regime. Corvane intends to claim the credit under the Service Level Agreement.

4.2 The claim must be lodged within thirty (30) days after the end of September.

## 5. Follow-up

5.1 Operations will compare this note with the root-cause report that Orion owes within ten (10) business days and raise any differences at the next quarterly service review.

5.2 Depot managers have been asked to keep manual check-in sheets for audit purposes for ninety (90) days.

## 6. Lessons for Corvane

6.1 Depot supervisors reported that the manual check-in sheets were hard to find. Operations will keep a laminated copy at each dispatch desk.

6.2 The duty manager recommends that the escalation contact list for Orion be reviewed every quarter and tested with a short call.
