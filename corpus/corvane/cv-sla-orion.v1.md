# Service Level Agreement — Orion Telematics Platform

This Service Level Agreement (the "SLA") sets out the availability and support commitments given by Orion Telematics Ltd. ("Orion") to Corvane Freight Holdings ("Corvane") for the Platform under the Master Services Agreement dated 1 March 2023.

## 1. Availability Commitment

1.1 Orion commits to a monthly Platform availability of 99.9%, measured across each calendar month at the Orion ingest gateway and excluding Scheduled Maintenance.

1.2 "Scheduled Maintenance" means maintenance carried out between 01:00 and 05:00 UTC on Sundays, up to a maximum of six (6) hours in any calendar month.

1.3 Availability is calculated as the total minutes in the month, less Scheduled Maintenance minutes and Downtime minutes, divided by the total minutes in the month less Scheduled Maintenance minutes.

## 2. Service Credits

2.1 If monthly availability falls below 99.9% but is at least 99.5%, Corvane is entitled to a service credit of five percent (5%) of the monthly platform fee for that month.

2.2 If monthly availability falls below 99.5% but is at least 99.0%, the service credit is ten percent (10%) of the monthly platform fee.

2.3 If monthly availability falls below 99.0%, the service credit is twenty percent (20%) of the monthly platform fee.

2.4 Corvane must claim a service credit in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the affected month. Credits are applied against the next invoice and are not payable in cash.

## 3. Support Response

3.1 Orion operates a support desk twenty-four (24) hours a day, every day of the year.

3.2 For a Severity 1 incident (Platform unavailable to all depots), Orion shall respond within 15 minutes of Corvane raising the incident and shall provide status updates at least every thirty (30) minutes until resolution.

3.3 For a Severity 2 incident (material degradation affecting some depots), Orion shall respond within two (2) hours and update at least every four (4) hours.

## 4. Incident Reporting

4.1 Orion shall deliver a written root-cause report within ten (10) business days after any Severity 1 incident, stating the start time, end time, cause, affected sites and corrective actions.

4.2 Where Corvane's records of an incident differ from Orion's report, the parties shall meet within ten (10) business days to reconcile the figures before any service credit is finalised.

## 5. Reviews

5.1 The parties shall hold a service review every quarter. Corvane's operations coordinator and Orion's account lead attend, and minutes are kept by Corvane.

5.2 Persistent failure, meaning availability below 99.0% in any three (3) months within a rolling six (6) month period, is a material breach for the purposes of the Master Services Agreement.
