# Revolving Credit Facility — Summary of Terms

This summary describes the senior revolving credit facility (the "Facility") agreed between Corvane Freight Holdings ("Corvane"), as borrower, and a syndicate led by Tessaly Commercial Bank plc (the "Agent"). It is a treasury working summary, not the executed agreement, which prevails in case of difference.

## 1. Commitment and Maturity

1.1 The total commitment under the Facility is USD 180,000,000, available in USD and in euro-equivalent drawings.

1.2 The Facility was signed on 30 June 2025 and matures on 30 June 2029. Corvane may request two one-year extensions, each subject to lender consent given no later than ninety (90) days before the relevant anniversary.

1.3 A sub-limit of USD 25,000,000 is available for letters of credit issued on behalf of Corvane's freight-forwarding subsidiaries.

## 2. Pricing

2.1 Drawn amounts bear interest at the reference rate plus a margin of 2.15% per annum, reset on each interest period.

2.2 Corvane pays a commitment fee of 0.35% per annum on the undrawn and uncancelled commitment, accruing daily and payable quarterly in arrears.

2.3 An arrangement fee of USD 1,260,000 was paid at signing and an annual agency fee of USD 85,000 is payable on each anniversary.

## 3. Financial Covenants

3.1 Corvane shall not permit consolidated net leverage, measured on a rolling twelve (12) month basis at each quarter end, to exceed 3.50x.

3.2 Corvane shall not permit consolidated interest cover to fall below 4.00x at any quarter end.

3.3 Corvane shall maintain minimum liquidity of USD 20,000,000, being unrestricted cash plus undrawn committed availability, at all times.

## 4. Reporting

4.1 Corvane delivers a compliance certificate within forty-five (45) days after each quarter end, signed by the Treasury Lead and the Group Chief Financial Officer.

4.2 Annual audited accounts are due within one hundred twenty (120) days after the financial year end.

## 5. Events of Default and Mandatory Prepayment

5.1 Failure to pay any amount when due is an event of default if not remedied within three (3) business days.

5.2 A covenant breach is an event of default unless remedied within fifteen (15) business days after the earlier of Corvane becoming aware of it and notice from the Agent.

5.3 On a change of control of Corvane, each lender may cancel its commitment and require prepayment of its share on thirty (30) days' notice.

## 6. Handling

6.1 This summary is restricted to the Treasury function. It must not be copied to vendors, auditors or other departments without the approval of the Treasury Lead.
