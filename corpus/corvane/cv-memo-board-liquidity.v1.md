# Board Finance Memo — Liquidity Outlook for Financial Year 2027

To: Board Finance Committee. From: Treasury Lead. Date: 15 September 2026. This memo gives the Treasury view of liquidity for the coming financial year and asks the Committee to note three planning assumptions.

## 1. Position

1.1 At 31 August 2026 unrestricted cash stood at USD 47,300,000 and undrawn committed availability was USD 112,000,000, giving total liquidity of USD 159,300,000.

1.2 Average daily unrestricted cash over the previous twelve (12) months was USD 41,800,000, and the lowest month-end balance was USD 33,100,000 in March 2026.

## 2. Planning Assumptions

2.1 Capital expenditure for financial year 2027 is budgeted at USD 64,000,000, of which USD 22,500,000 is for the replacement of refrigerated trailers.

2.2 Treasury assumes that freight volumes grow by three percent (3%) and that operating cash conversion stays within two percentage points of the current year.

2.3 No new long-term debt is planned. Treasury expects to repay USD 15,000,000 of drawings by the end of the first quarter of financial year 2027.

## 3. Risks

3.1 The principal liquidity risk is a slower collection cycle from two large retail customers, which together represent roughly nineteen percent (19%) of receivables.

3.2 A second risk is the concentration of cash in the two largest relationship banks, which is being reduced in line with the counterparty limit in the Treasury Policy.

## 4. Requests to the Committee

4.1 The Committee is asked to note the planning assumptions in Section 2.

4.2 The Committee is asked to confirm that quarterly liquidity reports continue in their present format and that the next report is due in December 2026.

## 5. Distribution

5.1 This memo is restricted to the Treasury function and Committee members. It contains figures that have not been disclosed to lenders or auditors.

## 6. Next Steps

6.1 Treasury will refresh the cash forecast monthly and circulate it to the Chief Financial Officer by the fifth (5th) business day of each month.

6.2 Treasury will review the timing of the trailer replacement programme with Operations and report any change in phasing that moves more than USD 5,000,000 between quarters.

6.3 Treasury will report back to the Committee on the outcome of the customer collection review at its next scheduled meeting.
