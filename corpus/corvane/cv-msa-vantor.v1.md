# Master Services Agreement — Vantor Dispatch Systems Ltd.

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Corvane Freight Holdings ("Corvane") and Vantor Dispatch Systems Ltd. ("Vantor") and takes effect on 1 June 2024 (the "Effective Date").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Platform" means the depot dispatch scheduling and driver-assignment service operated by Vantor and made available to Corvane depots under this Agreement.

1.2 "Fees" means the monthly platform fee and per-driver fees set out in the Vantor Rate Card, as updated by written schedule signed by both parties.

1.3 "Service Level Agreement" means the service level terms agreed between the parties for the Platform.

## 2. Term and Renewal

2.1 The initial term of this Agreement is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date.

2.2 This Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

2.3 A notice of non-renewal must be sent to the notice address stated in Section 8 and is effective on receipt by the other party.

## 3. Services and Service Levels

3.1 Vantor shall provide the Platform with a monthly availability of 99.7%, measured at the Vantor application gateway and excluding scheduled maintenance.

3.2 If monthly availability falls below 99.7%, Vantor shall credit four percent (4%) of the monthly platform fee for that month.

## 4. Payment

4.1 Vantor shall invoice the Fees monthly in advance. Corvane shall pay each undisputed invoice within net sixty (60) days of receipt.

4.2 Late payments accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month on the overdue amount, but only after Vantor has given Corvane ten (10) business days' written reminder.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

5.1 Subject to clause 5.2, the total aggregate liability of each party arising under this Agreement in any contract year shall not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the Fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

5.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or breach of the confidentiality obligations in Section 6.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret, use it only for this Agreement, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent duties.

## 7. Termination and Governing Law

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within forty-five (45) days of receiving notice describing the breach.

7.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Westmarch, and the courts of Westmarch have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.

## 8. Notices

8.1 Notices must be in writing and delivered to the legal department of the receiving party at its registered office, with a copy by email to the contract owner named in the Vantor Rate Card.
