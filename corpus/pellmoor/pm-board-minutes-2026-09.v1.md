# Board of Directors Minutes: 24 September 2026

Present: Chair Odalys Brennan; Directors Hamid Rostova, Ingrid Salgado, Petr Lindgaard, Maribel Achterberg, Tobias Wenham, Farida Okonjo. Also present: General Manager Corin Ashdown; Board Secretary Nadia Voss.

## 1. Open session

1.1 The Chair called the meeting to order at 18:00. The minutes of the 20 August 2026 meeting were approved without amendment.

1.2 The General Manager reported that treated water production for August averaged 94 megalitres per day, 3% below the five-year average, and that the Westfield pumping station had passed its commissioning tests.

1.3 The Board received the quarterly capital programme report. Spending to date is $11.2 million against an annual capital budget of $19.8 million.

## 2. Closed session

2.1 At 19:10 the Board went into closed session under its standing orders to consider a legal claim and a personnel matter. Only Directors, the General Manager and the Board Secretary remained.

2.2 The Board considered the claim by Larkspur Civil Works Ltd. arising from the 2024 Northgate trunk main project. The General Counsel advised that the claim, originally $1,240,000, carried a litigation risk the Authority should avoid.

2.3 On a motion by Director Salgado, seconded by Director Okonjo, the Board approved a confidential settlement of $865,000, to be paid from the Rate Stabilization Reserve. The motion was carried by a vote of 6 to 1, with Director Lindgaard against.

2.4 The General Manager is authorized to sign the settlement deed and a mutual non-disparagement undertaking. The terms are not to be disclosed outside the Board and the General Counsel's office.

2.5 The Board then considered a personnel matter concerning the vacant post of Chief Engineer and agreed that the Chair and the General Manager will interview two shortlisted candidates before 30 October 2026.

## 3. Return to open session

3.1 The Board returned to open session at 20:25 and the Chair reported that a closed-session matter had been resolved, without giving details.

3.2 The next regular meeting is on 22 October 2026 at 18:00.

## 4. Adjournment

4.1 There being no further business, the Chair adjourned the meeting at 20:30.

Minutes recorded by Nadia Voss, Board Secretary.
