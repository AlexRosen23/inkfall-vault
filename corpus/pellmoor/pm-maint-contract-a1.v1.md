# Amendment No. 1 to the Pump Station Maintenance Contract

## 1. Background

1.1 This amendment is made between Pellmoor Water Authority (the "Authority") and Harrowgate Pumpworks Ltd. ("Harrowgate") and amends the Pump Station Maintenance Contract that took effect on 1 March 2024 (the "Contract").

1.2 The Authority has commissioned a twelfth pumping station at Westfield, and the parties wish to extend the Contract to cover it, to reflect cost increases and to shorten emergency response.

## 2. Amendments

2.1 Clause 1.1 of the Contract is amended by replacing "eleven (11) pumping stations" with "twelve (12) pumping stations", and Schedule 1 is updated to add the Westfield station.

2.2 Clause 2.1 is amended so that the Contract continues until 28 February 2028.

2.3 Clause 3.1 is deleted and replaced with: "For an emergency callout, Harrowgate shall have a qualified technician on site within two (2) hours of the Authority's request, at any hour."

2.4 Clause 4.1 is amended by replacing "$412,000" with "$438,500". The new annual fee applies to instalments invoiced from 1 October 2025 and is otherwise payable as stated in clause 4.1.

## 3. Unchanged Terms

3.1 Clause 5 (Spare Parts) is not changed. In particular, the $25,000 quote threshold in clause 5.2 of the Contract continues to apply to Harrowgate's own purchases of spare parts.

3.2 The termination rights in clause 9 are not changed, and clause 9.2 now refers to the two (2) hour response time in this amendment.

3.3 Harrowgate shall bring the Westfield station into its quarterly inspection programme from the quarter beginning 1 January 2026 and shall give the Authority an asset list for the station within thirty (30) days of the date of this amendment.

3.4 Hourly rates in Schedule 2 are not changed by this amendment, and the rate-fixing period stated in clause 4.2 of the Contract has expired and is not extended.

## 4. General

4.1 This amendment takes effect on 1 October 2025.

4.2 Except as expressly amended here, the Contract remains in full force. If this amendment and the Contract conflict, this amendment prevails for the clauses it names.

4.3 The Authority's Director of Operations and Harrowgate's Managing Director signed this amendment on 12 September 2025.
