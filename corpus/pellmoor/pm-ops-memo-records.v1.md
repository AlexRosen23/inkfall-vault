# Operations Memo: Handling of Maintenance and Telemetry Records

From: Delia Ferreira, Operations Manager
To: All operations staff, pump station crews and control room operators
Date: 12 August 2024

## 1. Why this memo

1.1 The operations shared drive is at 91% capacity and the control room has asked for a simple rule on what can be cleared out each year. This memo gives that rule for the records the operations team creates every day.

1.2 It is guidance for day-to-day housekeeping within the operations department. It does not replace the Authority's corporate policies.

## 2. Maintenance records

2.1 Maintenance work orders, contractor invoices and callout sheets held by the operations department shall be kept for five (5) years from the end of the fiscal year in which they were closed, and may then be deleted from the shared drive by the stations supervisor.

2.2 Planned-maintenance checklists signed by the crew shall be kept for the same five (5) years and scanned at 300 dpi before the paper original is shredded.

2.3 Pump performance test reports are kept for the life of the pump plus two (2) years.

## 3. Telemetry and SCADA data

3.1 Raw SCADA historian data is kept online for thirty-six (36) months and then archived to cold storage. Archived data is not searchable and a restore takes up to three (3) working days.

3.2 Telemetry alarm logs are kept for twenty-four (24) months. The control room supervisor reviews the alarm logs each quarter and exports any log relating to a Severity 1 incident to the incident file before deletion.

3.3 Screenshots of the telemetry dashboard may be deleted after ninety (90) days unless they are attached to an incident note.

## 4. Who does what

4.1 The stations supervisor, Bram Oduya, is responsible for the annual clear-out of work orders and callout sheets, which shall be completed by the end of October each year.

4.2 The control room supervisor, Ines Valkenburg, is responsible for telemetry alarm logs and for keeping the register of exports to incident files.

4.3 Staff who are unsure whether a document is an operations record or a corporate record should ask the stations supervisor before deleting anything.

## 5. Questions

5.1 Please send questions about this memo to the Operations Manager. A revised memo will be issued if capacity or tooling changes.
