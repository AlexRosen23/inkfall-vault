# Restricted Board Resolution 2026-07: Westfield Reservoir Financing

Adopted: 16 July 2026
Moved by: Director Hamid Rostova
Seconded by: Director Maribel Achterberg

## 1. Recitals

1.1 The Board has approved the Westfield Reservoir Expansion as the principal capital project of the 2026 to 2031 capital plan, with a total estimated cost of $44.0 million.

1.2 The Chief Financial Officer has advised that $12.5 million can be met from the Capital Renewal Reserve and that the balance should be financed by borrowing.

## 2. Resolutions

2.1 The Authority is authorized to borrow up to $31,500,000 by way of a revenue-backed term loan for a period of not more than twenty-five (25) years.

2.2 The General Manager and the Chief Financial Officer are jointly authorized to negotiate the loan with up to three lenders and to sign a term sheet, but not the final loan agreement, which requires a further resolution of the Board.

2.3 The maximum interest rate that may be accepted is 5.75% a year, fixed or swapped to fixed, and the General Manager shall report to the Chair at once if no lender offers terms within that limit.

2.4 The Chief Financial Officer shall obtain an independent opinion from the Authority's financial adviser on the competitiveness of each lender's terms before any term sheet is signed, and shall give a copy of that opinion to every Director.

2.5 Proceeds of the loan may be used only for the Westfield Reservoir Expansion and its directly related pipework, pumping and treatment works, and may not be applied to operating costs.

## 3. Confidentiality

3.1 The terms of this resolution and the identity of any lender approached are restricted to Directors, the General Manager, the Chief Financial Officer, the General Counsel and the Board Secretary until a loan agreement is signed.

3.2 No public statement about the borrowing may be made before the Board has approved the wording of a notice to ratepayers.

## 4. Reporting

4.1 The Chief Financial Officer shall report to each regular Board meeting on progress with the loan negotiations until the loan agreement has been signed.

4.2 This resolution lapses on 31 December 2027 if no loan agreement has been signed by that date.

Certified as a true copy by Nadia Voss, Board Secretary.
