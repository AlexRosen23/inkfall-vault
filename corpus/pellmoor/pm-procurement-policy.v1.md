# Procurement Policy

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy governs how Pellmoor Water Authority (the "Authority") buys goods, services and construction. Its aims are fair competition, value for money and a clear audit trail for every purchase.

1.2 It applies to all purchases paid from the operating or capital budget, whether made by a department head, a project manager or the Procurement Office.

## 2. Purchasing Thresholds

2.1 Purchases up to $5,000 may be made by a budget holder using a purchasing card, with one documented price check.

2.2 Purchases above $5,000 and up to $25,000 require two written quotes, approved by the budget holder's department head.

2.3 Purchases above $25,000 and up to $100,000 require three written quotes, evaluated by the Procurement Office against criteria fixed before quotes are requested.

2.4 Purchases above $100,000 require a formal sealed bid process run by the Procurement Office, with a public notice period of at least twenty-one (21) days.

2.5 Any contract or purchase above $250,000 requires approval by the Board of Directors before award.

## 3. Emergency Purchases

3.1 The General Manager may authorize an emergency purchase of up to $60,000 without competition where delay would endanger public health, interrupt supply or cause serious damage to infrastructure.

3.2 The General Manager shall file a written report on each emergency purchase with the Procurement Office within ten (10) business days of the authorization.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

4.1 No employee may take part in evaluating or approving a purchase from a supplier in which the employee or a close relative holds a financial interest.

4.2 Evaluators shall sign a conflict-of-interest declaration before opening any quote or bid. Declarations are kept with the procurement file.

## 5. Splitting and Records

5.1 A purchase shall not be divided into parts to avoid a threshold in clause 2. Related purchases made from the same supplier within a twelve (12) month period are aggregated when applying the thresholds.

5.2 The Procurement Office shall keep the complete file for every purchase above $5,000, including quotes, evaluation sheets, award decisions and signed contracts, in line with the Records Retention Policy.

## 6. Review

6.1 The Procurement Office shall review this policy every two (2) years and recommend changes to the Chief Administrative Officer.

6.2 Thresholds in clause 2 are expressed in whole dollars and exclude any sales or use tax.
