# Records Retention Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This policy sets how long Pellmoor Water Authority (the "Authority") keeps its records, and when they must be destroyed. It applies to every employee, contractor and board member who creates or receives records on behalf of the Authority.

1.2 A "record" is any recorded information, in any format, that documents a decision, transaction or activity of the Authority. Convenience copies, drafts without approval and personal notes are not records unless a Records Administrator declares otherwise.

1.3 This version replaces the version that took effect on 1 March 2023 and applies to all records, including records created before its effective date that have not yet been destroyed.

## 2. Retention Periods

2.1 Financial records, including general ledgers, payment vouchers, supplier invoices, contractor invoices and maintenance work orders, shall be retained for seven (7) years from the end of the fiscal year in which they were created.

2.2 Customer billing and account records shall be retained for four (4) years after the account is closed.

2.3 Water quality laboratory records, including sampling logs, chain-of-custody forms and analytical results, shall be retained for ten (10) years from the date of sampling.

2.4 Personnel files shall be retained for six (6) years after the employee separates from the Authority.

2.5 Electronic mail is retained for two (2) years from the date of sending, unless the message has been filed as a record under clause 1.2.

## 3. Destruction

3.1 Records that have reached the end of their retention period shall be destroyed in a manner that makes reconstruction impracticable. Paper records are cross-cut shredded; electronic records are overwritten or cryptographically erased.

3.2 The Records Administrator shall sign a destruction certificate for each batch destroyed and keep the certificate for the life of the Authority. No batch may be destroyed without the written approval of the relevant department head.

3.3 Destruction shall be carried out in the first quarter of each calendar year, after the annual retention review described in clause 5.2.

## 4. Legal Holds

4.1 When the General Counsel's office issues a legal hold notice, destruction of every record covered by the notice is suspended immediately, regardless of any retention period in clause 2.

4.2 A legal hold remains in force until the General Counsel's office releases it in writing. The Records Administrator shall maintain a register of active holds and review it every six (6) months.

## 5. Responsibilities

5.1 Department heads are responsible for ensuring that their staff file records in the approved repositories and do not keep the only copy of a record on a personal device. Departmental memos may not shorten a period set in clause 2.

5.2 The Records Administrator shall carry out an annual retention review each January, report the results to the Chief Administrative Officer, and propose any amendments to this policy.

5.3 Questions about the classification or retention of a particular record should be directed to the Records Administrator before the record is moved or deleted.
