# Board of Directors Minutes: 18 December 2025

Present: Chair Odalys Brennan; Directors Hamid Rostova, Ingrid Salgado, Petr Lindgaard, Maribel Achterberg, Tobias Wenham, Farida Okonjo. Also present: General Manager Corin Ashdown; Board Secretary Nadia Voss.

## 1. Budget and rates

1.1 The Board adopted the 2026 operating budget of $64.3 million and approved an average rate increase of 4.2% effective 1 January 2026. The motion was moved by Director Rostova and carried unanimously.

1.2 The Board asked the General Manager to bring a revised drought contingency plan to its April 2026 meeting. No surcharge was discussed or approved at this meeting.

## 2. Contract reporting

2.1 Following a review of the 2025 audit findings, the Board resolved that the General Manager shall report every contract award above $75,000 to the Board at its next regular meeting, even where the award was within the General Manager's own authority.

2.2 This reporting duty is separate from, and in addition to, the requirement in the Procurement Policy that contracts above the board approval threshold receive Board approval before award.

2.3 The Board Secretary will keep a register of reported awards and circulate it to Directors each quarter.

## 3. Reserves

3.1 The Board confirmed the minimum balance of the Rate Stabilization Reserve at $4.5 million and the minimum balance of the Capital Renewal Reserve at $12 million.

3.2 The Chief Financial Officer reported that the Rate Stabilization Reserve stood at $6.1 million on 30 November 2025.

## 4. Closed session

4.1 The Board met in closed session from 19:40 to 20:10 to receive legal advice on a land easement dispute at the Southmere reservoir. No decision was taken and no minute of the advice is recorded here.

4.2 Directors were reminded that the content of closed-session advice is privileged and that Directors must not discuss it with staff or members of the public. Any Director who needs a copy of the advice may request it from the General Counsel.

## 5. Other business

5.1 The Chair thanked Director Wenham for chairing the audit subcommittee and noted that his term runs to 31 December 2027.

5.2 The next regular meeting was set for 22 January 2026 at 18:00, and the meeting was adjourned at 20:20.

Minutes recorded by Nadia Voss, Board Secretary.
