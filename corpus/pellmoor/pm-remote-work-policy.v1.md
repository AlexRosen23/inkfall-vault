# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This policy sets the rules for remote and hybrid work at Pellmoor Water Authority (the "Authority"). It takes effect on 1 January 2027 and is not in force before that date.

1.2 It applies to office-based staff whose duties can be performed away from an Authority site. It does not apply to control room operators, pump station crews, laboratory analysts or other roles that must be on site.

## 2. Hybrid Schedule

2.1 Eligible employees may work remotely up to three (3) days per week, with the remaining days spent at an Authority office, as agreed with their manager.

2.2 Core hours are 09:30 to 15:30, Monday to Friday. Employees shall be reachable by telephone or messaging during core hours.

2.3 A manager may require an employee to attend on a remote day with at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice for a meeting, an emergency or a training session.

## 3. Equipment and Allowance

3.1 The Authority shall provide a laptop, a headset and a secondary monitor to each eligible employee who works remotely.

3.2 Employees who work remotely at least two (2) days per week shall receive a home-working allowance of $45 per month, paid with salary.

## 4. Security and Records

4.1 Authority records shall be created and stored only in approved repositories, and not on personal devices or personal cloud accounts, in line with the Records Retention Policy.

4.2 Employees shall work from a private space where Authority information cannot be overheard or seen, and shall use the Authority's virtual private network at all times.

4.3 Employees shall report a lost or stolen device to the Information Technology help desk within two (2) hours of discovering the loss, so that remote access can be disabled.

## 5. Approval and Review

5.1 Remote work arrangements are approved by the employee's department head and recorded by Human Resources.

5.2 Arrangements are reviewed after six (6) months and may be withdrawn with thirty (30) days' notice if service levels or security rules are not met.

5.3 The Human Resources Director shall review this policy twelve (12) months after its effective date and report to the Chief Administrative Officer.
