# Pump Station Maintenance Contract

## 1. Scope

1.1 Harrowgate Pumpworks Ltd. ("Harrowgate") shall carry out planned and corrective maintenance of the mechanical and electrical equipment at the eleven (11) pumping stations listed in Schedule 1 for Pellmoor Water Authority (the "Authority").

1.2 Planned maintenance includes quarterly inspections, bearing and seal replacement, motor insulation testing and annual pump performance tests.

## 2. Term

2.1 The contract starts on 1 March 2024 and continues for thirty-six (36) months, ending on 28 February 2027, unless terminated earlier under clause 9.

## 3. Response Times

3.1 For an emergency callout, Harrowgate shall have a qualified technician on site within four (4) hours of the Authority's request, at any hour.

3.2 For a non-emergency corrective request, Harrowgate shall attend within three (3) business days.

## 4. Fees

4.1 The Authority shall pay an annual maintenance fee of $412,000, invoiced in four equal quarterly instalments, each payable within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice.

4.2 Corrective work outside the planned programme is charged at the hourly rates in Schedule 2, which are fixed until 28 February 2026.

## 5. Spare Parts

5.1 Harrowgate shall obtain the Authority's written approval before ordering any spare part costing more than $2,500.

5.2 Where a spare part or a package of related parts costs more than $25,000, Harrowgate shall obtain three written quotes and give them to the Authority, which shall choose the supplier.

5.3 Parts are the property of the Authority from the moment they are installed, and Harrowgate shall return removed parts on request.

## 6. Reporting

6.1 Harrowgate shall give the Authority a maintenance report within ten (10) business days of the end of each quarter, listing work done, parts replaced and defects found.

6.2 Harrowgate shall record each emergency callout in a work order that states the time of request, time of arrival and time of repair.

## 7. Insurance and Liability

7.1 Harrowgate shall keep public liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000 per claim for the whole term.

7.2 Harrowgate's liability for any one event is limited to $1,000,000, except for death, personal injury or deliberate default.

## 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this contract on ninety (90) days' written notice.

9.2 The Authority may terminate immediately if Harrowgate fails to meet the emergency response time in clause 3.1 on three (3) occasions in any twelve (12) month period.
