# Telemetry Service Level Agreement

## 1. Services

1.1 Tideline Telemetry Ltd. ("Tideline") shall provide remote telemetry gateways, a cellular and radio data network and a hosted monitoring dashboard to Pellmoor Water Authority (the "Authority") for its pumping stations, reservoirs and pressure zones.

1.2 The services cover the forty-two (42) monitored sites listed in Schedule A of the master agreement. Additional sites may be added by written order at the unit rate in Schedule B.

## 2. Fees

2.1 The Authority shall pay Tideline a monthly service fee of $18,200, invoiced on the first business day of each month and payable within thirty (30) days.

2.2 The fee is fixed for the first thirty-six (36) months of the agreement and may then be increased by no more than the annual regional consumer price index.

## 3. Availability

3.1 Tideline shall keep the telemetry network and dashboard available at least 99.5% of each calendar month, measured across all monitored sites and excluding scheduled maintenance.

3.2 Scheduled maintenance shall not exceed six (6) hours in any month, shall take place between 01:00 and 05:00 local time, and shall be announced at least seven (7) days in advance.

## 4. Incident Response

4.1 A Severity 1 incident is a loss of telemetry from three (3) or more sites, or from any site that serves a pump station above 20 megalitres per day. Tideline shall respond to a Severity 1 incident within thirty (30) minutes of notification, around the clock.

4.2 A Severity 2 incident is a loss of telemetry from one or two sites. Tideline shall respond within four (4) hours during business hours.

4.3 "Respond" means a named engineer has contacted the Authority's duty operator and started diagnosis. An automated acknowledgement does not count.

## 5. Service Credits

5.1 If monthly availability falls below the level in clause 3.1, the Authority is entitled to a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee for each full 0.5 percentage points of shortfall, up to a maximum of 25% of the monthly fee.

5.2 If Tideline misses the response time in clause 4.1, the Authority is entitled to a credit of 5% of the monthly fee for that incident.

5.3 Credits are applied to the next invoice and are the Authority's sole financial remedy for the failure concerned, without prejudice to the right to terminate under clause 6.

## 6. Reporting and Termination

6.1 Tideline shall deliver a written incident report within five (5) business days of every Severity 1 incident, stating the time the fault began, the time service was restored and the root cause.

6.2 Either party may terminate this agreement on one hundred and eighty (180) days' written notice. The Authority may terminate immediately if availability falls below 98.0% in any two consecutive months.
