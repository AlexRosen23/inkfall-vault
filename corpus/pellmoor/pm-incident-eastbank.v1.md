# Incident Note: Eastbank Pumping Station Telemetry Loss

Incident reference: OPS-2026-0314
Prepared by: Control Room, Pellmoor Water Authority
Status: Closed

## 1. Summary

1.1 Telemetry from the Eastbank pumping station and four neighbouring sites was lost from 02:10 on 14 March 2026. The control room declared a Severity 1 incident under the Telemetry Service Level Agreement.

1.2 Telemetry was fully restored at 05:35 on the same morning. Pumping continued under local automatic control throughout, and no customer lost supply.

## 2. Timeline

2.1 02:10 on 14 March 2026: the control room alarm for Eastbank shows "communications fail". The shift operator, Tamsin Reyes, confirms that five sites are affected and notes the time in the shift log.

2.2 02:16: Tamsin Reyes telephones the Tideline on-call number. The engineer returns the call at 02:29, which is within the fifteen minute response commitment only if the clock is taken from the second call attempt at 02:20.

2.3 03:05: a Harrowgate technician is requested to check the Eastbank radio mast and arrives at 04:40 after a road closure.

2.4 05:35: the gateway is restarted by Tideline and all five sites report normally.

## 3. Impact

3.1 Operators could not see tank levels or pump status at the five sites for three hours and twenty-five minutes. The Eastbank wet well was inspected manually by a crew at 03:50, and levels were normal.

3.2 No water quality alarms were raised and no regulatory notification was required.

## 4. Follow-up actions

4.1 The Director of Operations will ask Tideline for a formal Severity 1 report within five (5) business days.

4.2 The control room will add a printed contact sheet for the on-call engineer to each operator desk by 31 March 2026.
