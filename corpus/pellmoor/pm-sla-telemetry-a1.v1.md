# Amendment No. 1 to the Telemetry Service Level Agreement

## 1. Background

1.1 This amendment is made between Pellmoor Water Authority (the "Authority") and Tideline Telemetry Ltd. ("Tideline") and amends the Telemetry Service Level Agreement that took effect on 15 January 2024 (the "SLA").

1.2 Following three Severity 1 incidents in the second half of 2024, the parties agreed to tighten the response commitment for the most serious incidents. Nothing in this amendment changes the fees, the availability level or the termination rights in the SLA.

## 2. Amendments

2.1 Clause 4.1 of the SLA is deleted and replaced with the following: "A Severity 1 incident is a loss of telemetry from three (3) or more sites, or from any site that serves a pump station above 20 megalitres per day. Tideline shall respond to a Severity 1 incident within fifteen (15) minutes of notification, around the clock."

2.2 Clause 4.2 of the SLA is amended by replacing "four (4) hours during business hours" with "two (2) hours during business hours".

2.3 A new clause 4.4 is added: "Tideline shall maintain a named on-call engineer for the Authority at all times and shall give the Authority's duty operator a direct telephone number for that engineer."

## 3. Service Credits

3.1 Clause 5.2 of the SLA continues to apply to the shortened response time in clause 2.1 of this amendment, so that a missed Severity 1 response earns a credit of 5% of the monthly fee for that incident.

3.2 Tideline shall not claim that the shortened response time is a change in scope entitling it to an increase in the monthly fee of $18,200.

## 4. General

4.1 This amendment takes effect on 1 April 2025 and applies to every incident notified on or after that date.

4.2 Except as expressly amended here, the SLA remains in full force. If this amendment and the SLA conflict, this amendment prevails for the clauses it names.

4.3 This amendment is signed for the Authority by the Director of Operations and for Tideline by its Regional Account Director. It may be signed in counterparts.
