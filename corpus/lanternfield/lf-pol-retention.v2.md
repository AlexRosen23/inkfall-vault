# Records Retention Policy (Revised)

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This policy sets out how long Lanternfield Partners LLP (the "Firm") keeps client files and business records and how they are destroyed. It applies to all lawyers, staff and contractors of the Firm and replaces the policy that took effect on 1 June 2024.

1.2 It is published so that clients can see how their files are handled once a matter ends.

## 2. Client Files

2.1 The Firm keeps each closed client file for ten (10) years after the date on which the matter is closed.

2.2 The closing date is the date recorded on the closure checklist and not the date of the last invoice.

2.3 Original documents belonging to the client are returned at closure, and the Firm keeps copies as part of the file.

2.4 Files already in the archive on 1 March 2027 are kept for ten (10) years from their closing date, unless they have already been destroyed.

## 3. Business Records

3.1 Accounting and billing records are kept for seven (7) years after the end of the financial year to which they relate.

3.2 Personnel records are kept for six (6) years after a person leaves the Firm.

3.3 Conflict-check records and engagement letters are kept for as long as the related client file is kept, and are destroyed with that file.

3.4 Records of complaints against the Firm are kept for six (6) years after the complaint is resolved, so that the Firm can show how it dealt with the matter.

## 4. Destruction

4.1 When a retention period ends, the records administrator will prepare a list of files due for destruction and send it to the responsible partner for approval.

4.2 Destruction must be recorded on a destruction certificate signed by the Records Administrator and countersigned by a partner. The certificate is kept permanently.

4.3 Paper files are shredded by an approved contractor, and electronic files are deleted from all systems, including backups, within sixty (60) days after approval.

## 5. Legal Holds

5.1 No record may be destroyed while a legal hold applies to it. A hold is released only by the partner who issued it, in writing.

## 6. Review

6.1 The finance partner will review this policy each year and report any proposed change to the partners.

6.2 This revised policy takes effect on 1 March 2027.
