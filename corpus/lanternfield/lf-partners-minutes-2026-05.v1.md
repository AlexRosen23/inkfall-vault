# Minutes of the Partners' Meeting — 20 May 2026

## 1. Attendance and Quorum

1.1 The partners of Lanternfield Partners LLP (the "Firm") met on 20 May 2026. Marcus Oyelaran chaired the meeting, and a quorum of four (4) partners was present throughout.

1.2 These minutes are restricted to the partners. They record decisions on matters of Firm governance and finances that are not to be shared with staff.

## 2. Client Rates

2.1 The partners considered the rate review for Calderwood Shipping Ltd. and resolved that the partner rate for the Calderwood engagement shall be $650 per hour with effect from 1 June 2026.

2.2 Mr Oyelaran will tell the client of the change in writing. The partners noted that the associate and analyst rates would not change in this review.

## 3. Insurance

3.1 The partners resolved to renew the Firm's professional indemnity insurance for the policy year starting 1 July 2026 with a limit of $20,000,000 per claim and an excess of $75,000.

3.2 The finance partner will report to the next meeting on whether the premium of $184,000 justifies a higher limit for the following year.

## 4. Records Retention

4.1 The partners approved in principle a revision of the Records Retention Policy to extend the retention period for closed client files from seven (7) years to ten (10) years, to take effect on 1 March 2027.

4.2 The records administrator will prepare the revised policy and a transition plan for files already in the archive.

## 5. Capital and Distributions

5.1 The partners approved a capital call of $40,000 per equity partner, payable by 30 June 2026, to fund the migration of the Firm's document store.

5.2 The interim distribution for the quarter ended 30 April 2026 was approved at $95,000 per equity partner.

## 6. Any Other Business

6.1 The partners noted that the lease on the fourth floor at Ashgrove Square expires on 31 December 2028 and asked the managing partner to open negotiations during 2027.

6.2 The next partners' meeting is fixed for 16 September 2026.

6.3 The partners thanked the retiring office manager, Odile Prentiss, for eleven years of service and agreed a leaving gift budget of $1,200.

6.4 The meeting closed at 6:40 p.m. The minutes were taken by the managing partner's assistant and approved by the chair on 27 May 2026.
