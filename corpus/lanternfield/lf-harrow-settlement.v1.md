# Memo — Harrow Estates Ltd. v. Fenwick Builders Ltd.: Settlement Strategy

## 1. Privileged and Confidential

1.1 This memo is prepared for the purposes of the Harrow matter and is protected by legal professional privilege. It is restricted to the partners and lawyers on the Harrow team. Access by anyone screened from the matter is prohibited.

1.2 It records the client's instructions of 15 September 2026 and the supervising partner's assessment of the claim by Harrow Estates Ltd. ("Harrow") against Fenwick Builders Ltd. ("Fenwick") for defective construction at Harrow Wharf.

## 2. The Claim

2.1 Harrow claims $2,900,000 for remedial works and lost rental income arising from defects in the podium slab and waterproofing at Harrow Wharf, completed in 2021.

2.2 We assess Harrow's prospects of establishing liability at about fifty-five percent (55%), with the principal risk being the expert evidence on causation of the slab cracking.

## 3. Settlement Authority

3.1 The Harrow board has authorised a settlement of up to $1,150,000, inclusive of interest and exclusive of costs. Any figure above that amount requires a fresh board resolution.

3.2 The opening offer to Fenwick will be $620,000. The team is not to disclose the settlement authority to Fenwick, to the mediator or to the insurer of Fenwick.

3.3 Costs to trial are estimated at $480,000, which the client has accepted as a factor in favour of settlement.

## 4. Mediation

4.1 Mediation is fixed for 14 December 2026 before Ines Valcourt at the Calder Dispute Centre in Ashgrove. The mediator's fee is shared equally between the parties.

4.2 The client's representative at mediation will be its development director, Callum Aldous, with authority limited to the amount in clause 3.1.

## 5. Next Steps

5.1 The team will serve the revised expert report by 30 October 2026 and will prepare a mediation position paper by 27 November 2026.

5.2 The supervising partner will review this memo again after the exchange of position papers and will update the settlement authority only on written instructions from the client.

5.3 This memo must not be forwarded outside the Harrow team and must be stored only in the Harrow matter workspace.
