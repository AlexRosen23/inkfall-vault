# Amendment No. 1 to Engagement Letter — Calderwood Shipping Ltd.

## 1. Background

1.1 Lanternfield Partners LLP (the "Firm") and Calderwood Shipping Ltd. (the "Client") are parties to the renewed engagement letter that took effect on 1 September 2025 (the "Engagement Letter").

1.2 The Client's finance team asked for a longer period in which to check the Firm's invoices against its internal purchase approvals. The Firm has agreed to the change set out in this Amendment No. 1 (the "Amendment").

## 2. Amendment of Clause 4.3

2.1 With effect from 1 June 2026 (the "Amendment Date"), clause 4.3 of the Engagement Letter is deleted and replaced with the following: "The Client may dispute an invoice by written notice to the Firm within twenty-one (21) days after the invoice date. The Client shall pay any undisputed part of the invoice on time."

2.2 The new period applies to every invoice dated on or after the Amendment Date. Invoices dated before the Amendment Date remain subject to the period stated in the original clause 4.3.

## 3. No Other Change

3.1 Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Engagement Letter continues in full force and effect. In particular, the hourly rates, the retainer, the payment period of twenty (20) days in clause 4.2 and the termination provisions are unchanged.

3.2 If this Amendment conflicts with the Engagement Letter, this Amendment prevails as to clause 4.3 only.

## 4. Notification

4.1 The Firm will print the new dispute period on every invoice issued after the Amendment Date, and the supervising partner, Marcus Oyelaran, will confirm the change to the Client's finance director, Rosalind Achterberg, in writing.

4.2 The Firm's billing manager will update the Client's billing profile so that the dispute deadline shown on each invoice is calculated from the invoice date and not from the date of delivery.

## 5. General

5.1 This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Calder.

5.2 This Amendment may be signed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.

5.3 A copy of this Amendment must be filed with the Engagement Letter and kept with it under the Firm's records policy.

5.4 Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Engagement Letter.
