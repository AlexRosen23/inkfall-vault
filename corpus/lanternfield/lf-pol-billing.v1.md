# Billing and Collections Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This policy sets out how Lanternfield Partners LLP (the "Firm") issues invoices, handles client queries and writes off unpaid fees. It applies to every client engagement, whatever its terms say about billing, and to every timekeeper and member of the finance team.

1.2 The Firm's billing manager is responsible for the day-to-day operation of this policy. The finance partner is responsible for reviewing it each year.

## 2. Invoicing

2.1 Invoices are issued monthly, no later than the tenth (10th) business day of the month following the work.

2.2 Each invoice must itemise time by timekeeper, state the hourly rate applied and show the balance of any retainer held for the client.

2.3 The supervising partner must approve each invoice before it is sent. No invoice may be sent with a narrative that discloses privileged information about another client.

## 3. Client Queries and Disputes

3.1 A client may query or dispute an invoice by written notice within thirty (30) days after the invoice date. The billing manager will acknowledge the query within two (2) business days and tell the supervising partner.

3.2 Disputes raised after that period will be considered at the discretion of the supervising partner, who must record the reasons for the decision on the client file.

3.3 The undisputed part of an invoice remains payable on its due date.

## 4. Write-Offs

4.1 Amounts not exceeding $500 on a single invoice may be written off by the billing manager.

4.2 Amounts above $500 and up to $10,000 require the written approval of the supervising partner.

4.3 Amounts above $10,000 require the written approval of two partners, one of whom must be the finance partner.

## 5. Overdue Accounts

5.1 The billing manager will send a reminder when an invoice is fifteen (15) days overdue and escalate to the supervising partner when it is forty-five (45) days overdue.

5.2 The Firm may suspend work on an engagement when an invoice is sixty (60) days overdue, after the supervising partner has spoken to the client.

## 6. Records

6.1 Billing records, write-off approvals and dispute correspondence are kept under the Records Retention Policy.

6.2 This policy takes effect on 1 February 2025 and replaces the earlier billing guidance issued to staff in 2023.
