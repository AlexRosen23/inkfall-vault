# Engagement Letter — Calderwood Shipping Ltd.

## 1. Scope of Engagement

1.1 Lanternfield Partners LLP (the "Firm") agrees to act for Calderwood Shipping Ltd. (the "Client") in connection with the negotiation and review of charter, port-services and bunkering agreements entered into by the Client in the State of Calder (the "Engagement").

1.2 The Engagement does not include litigation, tax advice or regulatory filings. Any additional work requires a written variation signed by a partner of the Firm and by the Client.

## 2. Staffing and Rates

2.1 Marcus Oyelaran will supervise the Engagement. The Firm charges the following hourly rates: partners at $620 per hour, associates at $395 per hour and analysts at $210 per hour.

2.2 Rates apply from 15 January 2024 and may be reviewed by the Firm once in each calendar year on thirty (30) days' written notice to the Client.

## 3. Retainer

3.1 The Client shall pay an advance retainer of $25,000 on signing this letter. The Firm holds the retainer in its client account and applies it against invoices.

3.2 If the retainer balance falls below $5,000, the Firm may ask the Client to restore it to $25,000 before further work is done.

## 4. Billing and Payment

4.1 The Firm will invoice monthly in arrears, itemising time by timekeeper and describing the work done.

4.2 The Client shall pay each invoice within twenty (20) days after the invoice date.

4.3 The Client may dispute an invoice by written notice to the Firm within fourteen (14) days after the invoice date. The Client shall pay any undisputed part of the invoice on time.

## 5. Conflicts and Confidentiality

5.1 The Firm has searched its records and has found no conflict of interest. It will tell the Client at once if one arises and, if it cannot be managed, the Firm may need to stop acting.

5.2 The Firm shall keep the Client's information confidential and shall not use it for any other client.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

6.1 The Firm's total liability to the Client arising from the Engagement is limited to $2,000,000, except where the law does not permit a limit.

## 7. Termination

7.1 The Client may end the Engagement at any time by giving fourteen (14) days' written notice. The Firm may end it by giving thirty (30) days' written notice.

7.2 On termination the Client shall pay for work done and expenses incurred up to the end of the notice period.

## 8. File Retention

8.1 The Firm will keep the Engagement file for seven (7) years after the Engagement ends and will then destroy it under the Firm's records policy unless the Client asks for its return.

8.2 This letter is governed by the laws of the State of Calder.
