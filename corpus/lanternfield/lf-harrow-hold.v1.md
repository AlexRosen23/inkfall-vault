# Document Preservation Notice — Harrow Matter

## 1. Notice of Hold

1.1 Lanternfield Partners LLP (the "Firm") gives notice that, with effect from 31 August 2026 (the "Hold Date"), a litigation hold applies to all documents and data relating to the Harrow Estates Ltd. v. Fenwick Builders Ltd. proceedings (the "Harrow Matter").

1.2 This notice is restricted to the custodians named in Schedule A and to the supervising partner. It must not be shared with any person screened from the Harrow Matter.

## 2. What Must Be Preserved

2.1 Custodians must preserve every document created or received between 1 January 2022 and 30 August 2026 that concerns the design, construction, inspection or remediation of Harrow Wharf.

2.2 Documents include emails, drafts, text messages, voice notes, site photographs, expert working papers and handwritten notes, wherever stored, including on personal devices used for Firm business.

2.3 Custodians must not delete, alter or move preserved documents, even if they believe a document is duplicated elsewhere.

## 3. Systems Action

3.1 The records administrator will suspend all automatic deletion and mailbox archiving for the custodians within two (2) business days after the Hold Date and will confirm completion in writing to the supervising partner.

3.2 The information technology team will take a forensic copy of each custodian's mailbox and matter workspace before 30 September 2026.

## 4. Review and Release

4.1 The supervising partner will review the scope of this hold every ninety (90) days and will record the outcome on the matter file.

4.2 The hold continues until the supervising partner releases it in writing. Closure of the Harrow Matter does not release the hold automatically.

## 5. Acknowledgement

5.1 Each custodian must sign and return the acknowledgement form within five (5) business days after receiving this notice.

5.2 A custodian who becomes aware of lost or damaged material must tell the supervising partner immediately and must not attempt to recover it without instructions.

## 6. Schedule A: Custodians

6.1 The custodians are the five (5) lawyers and two (2) paralegals assigned to the Harrow Matter on the Hold Date, and the records administrator for the purposes of clause 3.1 only.
