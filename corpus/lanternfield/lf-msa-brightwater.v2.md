# Amended and Restated Master Supply Agreement — Brightwater Dairy Cooperative and Ostrander Packaging Ltd.

## 1. Parties and Term

1.1 This Amended and Restated Master Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 1 July 2025 between Brightwater Dairy Cooperative, a cooperative registered in the State of Calder ("Brightwater"), and Ostrander Packaging Ltd., a company incorporated in the State of Calder ("Ostrander"). It replaces the agreement made on 1 March 2023.

1.2 The Agreement commences on 1 July 2025 (the "Restatement Date") and continues until 28 February 2028. It then renews for successive terms of twelve (12) months unless terminated under Section 8.

## 2. Supply of Products

2.1 Ostrander shall supply insulated milk cartons, lids and shipping crates (the "Products") in the quantities stated in each purchase order issued by Brightwater (a "Purchase Order").

2.2 Brightwater shall purchase not fewer than 4,500,000 cartons in each contract year (the "Minimum Annual Volume"). A contract year runs from 1 March to the last day of February.

2.3 Ostrander shall deliver Products to the Brightwater depot at Millrace Quay within ten (10) business days after receipt of a Purchase Order.

2.4 Brightwater may reject defective Products by written notice given within fourteen (14) days after delivery, and Ostrander shall replace rejected Products at its own cost.

## 3. Prices and Payment

3.1 The price is $0.21 per carton (the "Unit Price"). The Unit Price may be reviewed on each anniversary of the Restatement Date, and no review may increase it by more than five percent (5%).

3.2 Ostrander shall invoice monthly in arrears. Brightwater shall pay each valid invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

3.3 Overdue amounts bear interest at 1.0% per month from the due date until the date of payment.

## 4. Warranties

4.1 Ostrander warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for eighteen (18) months after delivery and will comply with the food-contact standards in force in the State of Calder.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

5.1 Subject to clause 5.2, the total aggregate liability of each party to the other arising under this Agreement in any contract year shall not exceed $2,400,000 (the "Liability Cap").

5.2 The Liability Cap does not apply to death or personal injury caused by negligence, to fraud, or to a breach of Section 6.

5.3 Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss or for loss of profit.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each party shall keep the terms of this Agreement and all Purchase Orders confidential for five (5) years after the end of the Agreement, except where disclosure is required by law.

## 7. Governing Law

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Calder. The Commercial Division sitting in Ashgrove has exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.

## 8. Termination

8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving ninety (90) days' written notice to the other.

8.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days after written notice of the breach.

8.3 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered to the registered office of the recipient.
