# Memo — Brightwater Supply Agreement: Key Terms Quick Reference

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This memo was prepared by the Commercial Practice Group on 10 March 2026 for the Brightwater matter file. It gives the team a one-page reference to the supply relationship between Brightwater Dairy Cooperative and Ostrander Packaging Ltd. so that junior colleagues do not need to open the full agreement for routine questions.

1.2 It is a working summary only. If anything here differs from the signed documents, the signed documents control, and the matter team should say so to the supervising partner.

## 2. Summary of Terms

2.1 The Amended and Restated Master Supply Agreement took effect on 1 July 2025 and runs until 28 February 2028, renewing for twelve-month terms afterwards.

2.2 Termination for convenience: either party must give sixty (60) days' written notice. The team should diarise the notice window ahead of the 2028 renewal.

2.3 Payment: Brightwater now pays Ostrander's invoices within forty-five (45) days under the First Amendment, which took effect on 1 February 2026.

2.4 The Liability Cap and the Unit Price are unchanged from the 2025 restatement, and the five percent price-review ceiling continues to apply on each anniversary.

## 3. Operational Notes

3.1 Deliveries go to the depot at Millrace Quay. Purchase Orders are issued by Brightwater's procurement lead, Tobias Renwick, and acknowledged by Ostrander's account manager, Ilse Varga, within two (2) business days.

3.2 Defect claims must be raised within fourteen (14) days after delivery. The team has seen three late defect claims since January 2026 and has reminded Brightwater's depot staff of the deadline.

3.3 Ostrander has asked whether it may assign receivables arising under the agreement to a lender. The agreement is silent on assignment of receivables, and no consent has been requested or given.

## 4. Open Items

4.1 Confirm with the client whether it wants to renegotiate the Minimum Annual Volume before the 2027 contract year begins on 1 March 2027.

4.2 Prepare a short briefing note on the interaction between the First Amendment and the interest clause so that billing queries can be answered consistently.

4.3 Check that the signed copy of the First Amendment is on file and that the counterpart signed by Ostrander has been received.

## 5. Contacts

5.1 Client contact at Brightwater: Tobias Renwick, general manager. Supplier contact at Ostrander: Ilse Varga, managing director. Firm contacts: the supervising partner and the commercial analyst assigned to the matter.

5.2 This memo should be reviewed whenever an amendment is signed and in any event before 1 September 2026.
