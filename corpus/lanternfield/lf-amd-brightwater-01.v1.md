# First Amendment to the Amended and Restated Master Supply Agreement — Brightwater and Ostrander

## 1. Background

1.1 Brightwater Dairy Cooperative ("Brightwater") and Ostrander Packaging Ltd. ("Ostrander") are parties to the Amended and Restated Master Supply Agreement dated 1 July 2025 (the "Agreement").

1.2 Brightwater has changed its treasury cycle and asked for a longer period to pay Ostrander's monthly invoices. Ostrander has agreed on the terms of this First Amendment (the "Amendment").

## 2. Amendment of Clause 3.2

2.1 With effect from 1 February 2026 (the "Amendment Date"), clause 3.2 of the Agreement is deleted and replaced with the following: "Ostrander shall invoice monthly in arrears. Brightwater shall pay each valid invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt."

2.2 The change in clause 2.1 applies to every invoice received by Brightwater on or after the Amendment Date. Invoices received before the Amendment Date remain payable within the period stated in the original clause 3.2.

## 3. Interest

3.1 Clause 3.3 of the Agreement is not amended. Interest on overdue amounts continues to run from the due date, and the due date is the last day of the payment period in clause 3.2 as replaced by this Amendment.

## 4. Consideration

4.1 Each party confirms that the mutual promises in the Agreement and in this Amendment are sufficient consideration for the change, and neither party owes the other any additional fee in connection with it.

## 5. General

5.1 Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement continues in full force and effect, including the Unit Price, the Minimum Annual Volume, the Liability Cap and the notice period for termination for convenience.

5.2 References in the Agreement to "this Agreement" are to the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

5.3 If this Amendment conflicts with the Agreement, this Amendment prevails to the extent of the conflict, and only as to clause 3.2.

5.4 This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Calder, and the Commercial Division sitting in Ashgrove has exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.

5.5 This Amendment may be signed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument. It was signed for Brightwater by its general manager, Tobias Renwick, and for Ostrander by its managing director, Ilse Varga.

## 6. Notices and Records

6.1 Brightwater shall direct the first invoice it pays under the new period to its accounts payable team in Ashgrove and shall confirm the due date in writing to Ostrander within five (5) business days after receipt of the invoice.

6.2 A copy of this Amendment must be filed with the Agreement and kept with it for the same retention period.
