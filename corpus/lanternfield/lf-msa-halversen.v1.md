# Master Supply Agreement — Halversen Creamery Ltd. and Quillon Cartons Ltd.

## 1. Parties and Term

1.1 This Master Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 1 May 2024 between Halversen Creamery Ltd., a company incorporated in the State of Calder ("Halversen"), and Quillon Cartons Ltd., a company incorporated in the State of Calder ("Quillon").

1.2 The Agreement commences on 1 May 2024 and continues for an initial term of four (4) years. It then renews for successive terms of twelve (12) months unless terminated under Section 7.

## 2. Supply of Products

2.1 Quillon shall supply paperboard cartons, lids and shipping crates (the "Products") in the quantities stated in each purchase order issued by Halversen.

2.2 Halversen shall purchase not fewer than 3,000,000 cartons in each contract year (the "Minimum Annual Volume"). A contract year runs from 1 May to 30 April.

2.3 Quillon shall deliver Products to the Halversen plant at Fenmoor Wharf within twelve (12) business days after receipt of a purchase order.

## 3. Prices and Payment

3.1 The price is $0.17 per carton (the "Unit Price"). The Unit Price may be reviewed on each anniversary of 1 May 2024, and no review may increase it by more than three percent (3%).

3.2 Quillon shall invoice monthly in arrears. Halversen shall pay each valid invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

3.3 Overdue amounts bear interest at 1.5% per month from the due date until the date of payment.

## 4. Limitation of Liability

4.1 Subject to clause 4.2, the total aggregate liability of each party to the other arising under this Agreement in any contract year shall not exceed $1,800,000 (the "Liability Cap").

4.2 The Liability Cap does not apply to death or personal injury caused by negligence, to fraud, or to a breach of Section 5.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party shall keep the terms of this Agreement confidential for three (3) years after the end of the Agreement, except where disclosure is required by law.

## 6. Governing Law

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Calder. The Commercial Division sitting in Tallmere has exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.

## 7. Termination

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving forty-five (45) days' written notice to the other.

7.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within twenty-one (21) days after written notice of the breach.
